## Read the figures correctly

The dashboard labels its green card “Weekly budget,” but the displayed amount is a meal-plan estimate. It should not be read as actual spending. Budget and Reports show a fictional monthly allowance of ₪300, recorded spending of ₪12.50, and a remaining ₪287.50. The shopping list's ₪55 is an estimate, not another purchase. An older fictional purchase in history falls outside that monthly period.

Receipt lines were prepared as demo records. The captures show the real receipt-management UI, not a new OCR accuracy test or the full pending hybrid-review workflow. The confirmed receipt screen retains “Will be added to inventory” wording even after confirmation; it should be read alongside the confirmed-state message.

The images do not establish automatic low-stock notification generation, complete Arabic localization, or public production availability. No raw receipt photograph, credentials, real personal records, internal paths, or private validation artifacts are included.
